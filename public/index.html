Citizen Charter & Statutory Resolution Timelines / नागरिक अधिकार पत्र

Under the Public Service Delivery Guarantee Act and DARPG Guidelines, every citizen has the fundamental right to time-bound disposal of public grievances. Below are the statutory Service Level Agreements (SLAs) enforced by the National Grievance Redressal Core Engine.

Statutory SLA Norms by Administrative Department

Department Typical Grievances Handled Target SLA Hours Statutory Escalation Officer Compliance Standard
Dept. of Water Resources & Sanitation Main pipeline burst, water contamination, acute drinking water scarcity, low pressure 24 Hours Superintending Engineer (Water Supply) Mandatory water quality lab re-test
Municipal Solid Waste Management Dept. Uncollected community bins, open dumping, biomedical waste, stench hazard 48 Hours Chief Medical Officer of Health Photographic clearing proof mandatory
Roads, Highways & Pavement Infrastructure Dangerous potholes, collapsed manholes, road surface erosion, drainage overflow 72 Hours Executive Engineer (Public Works) Cold-mix/hot-mix patch verification
State Electricity & Power Distribution Co. Low-hanging live wire, damaged transformer, voltage fluctuation, power outage 12 Hours Assistant Divisional Engineer (Distribution) Immediate grid isolation within 2h
Public Health & Municipal Clinic Administration Clinic sanitation failure, mosquito breeding, medicine stockout, epidemic alert 24 Hours District Health Officer Vector fogging & medical dispatch

4-Tier Administrative Escalation Matrix (त्रि-स्तरीय अपीलीय पदानुक्रम)

LEVEL 1
Ward Nodal Officer
Primary recipient of complaint. Responsible for immediate field dispatch and initial Action Taken Report within SLA.
LEVEL 2
Zonal Executive Engineer
Auto-escalated if SLA breaches by 100%. Authority to reallocate funds and deploy emergency contractors.
LEVEL 3
Municipal Commissioner
Review at weekly District Grievance Redressal Committee (DGRC). Disciplinary remarks recorded in officer dossier.
LEVEL 4
State Lokayukta / Appellate Tribunal
Independent statutory judicial authority for chronic failure, malfeasance, or persistent non-compliance.

Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 Compliance Statement

1. Local Edge & Air-Gapped Inference: All natural language parsing and inference occurs strictly within sovereign, on-premise infrastructure. No grievance data is transmitted to third-party commercial LLM endpoints or external foreign cloud servers.

2. Zero Citizen PII in Embeddings: Personally Identifiable Information (Names, Phone Numbers, Aadhaar reference) is stripped prior to embedding calculation. Only anonymized incident descriptions are processed by vector indices.

3. Right to Correction & Human Redressal: In accordance with Section 12 of the DPDP Act, every automated classification can be contested by the citizen or manually overridden by the presiding Nodal Officer at any phase during the redressal lifecycle.
